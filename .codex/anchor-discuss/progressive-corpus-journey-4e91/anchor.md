# Anchor

## Original request (verbatim)
> This is just a random idea, but what if we allow people to embark on a kind of choose-your-own-adventure type of thing? When they are first presented on the home screen, instead of just seeing "My Work," "My Projects," and stuff, it has different directions of stuff you can learn about me. As you progress through each stage, you can choose different things that you liked or disliked, or maybe choose from an array of things that you want to learn about next that are all related and kind of flow together.
>
> Piece by piece, it builds this index without removing to a new page. We just keep the same page and just keep adding stuff to it, scrolling dynamically and arranging it in interesting ways so that it builds a comprehensive story while weaving together all my experiences and projects and all that stuff that is About Me, including my notes and things like that.

## Domain
- Product / interaction direction

## Starting intent
- Explore a choose-your-own-adventure portfolio in which visitors follow meaningful directions through Karthik's work and thinking rather than browse conventional content categories.
- Keep the experience on one continuously growing page: each choice adds the next part of the story instead of navigating away or replacing everything.
- Let the visitor's choices shape a coherent path through real experiences, projects, notes, and other About material.
- Allow the accumulated page to become both a personalized story and an index of what the visitor has uncovered.

## Goals
- Turn discovery of the corpus into an active, compelling experience rather than a static list or single-note preview.
- Give visitors several related, intelligible ways forward at each stage.
- Use progressive addition, scrolling, and changing spatial arrangement to make the site feel dynamic.
- Let a path cross work, projects, experiences, ideas, and exact notes when that strengthens the story; topical coherence is more important than forced cross-domain breadth.
- Preserve enough continuity that a visitor understands why each newly revealed piece follows from the one before it.

## Non-goals
- Do not turn the experience into freeform chat.
- Do not make it feel like a questionnaire, onboarding wizard, personality test, or conventional faceted filter.
- Do not build another dense mind map or node graph.
- Do not use arbitrary randomness as a substitute for meaningful relationships.
- Do not reduce the corpus to generic AI summaries or repeatedly surface the same note.
- Do not implement the idea until its core interaction and relationship to earlier constraints are consciously chosen.

## Constraints and taste
- The surface should remain radically minimalist, fast, and composed around substantial negative space.
- The content must be real: actual work, actual projects, actual notes, and specific receipts rather than abstract dots or vague generated claims.
- The AI behavior must provide genuine judgment or curation, not merely add an AI aesthetic.
- TypeSafe should be considered from the foundation as a typed decision layer for ranking or selecting the next meaningful directions.
- The site must not present a freeform prompt or chat transcript.
- Newly revealed material stays on the same page and builds cumulatively.
- The visual system may repaint, move, and rearrange, but accumulated information must remain readable and navigable.
- The accepted Solar Shadow daytime atmosphere and Winter Branch night condition remain the current environmental language unless Karthik consciously replaces them.
- Four plain topical directions replace the previous portfolio-category menu: AI, Research, Products, and Founders. They should read instantly, without thematic or AI-written setup copy.
- Choosing a direction is itself the visitor's interest signal. Do not add explicit like/dislike controls.
- The journey should form one coherent path through the corpus, not attempt to expose everything or grow into a complete corpus map.
- A topical path may use a deliberately narrow editorial lane when that is more truthful than broad semantic retrieval. In particular, Founders should focus primarily on BuildPurdue and Repple founder material.

## Conscious direction change — 2026-09-19
- Karthik explicitly rejected poetic or thematic opening sentences as AI-sounding and replaced them with the short topical starts AI, Research, Products, and Founders.
- Karthik explicitly corrected the Founders path after it surfaced unrelated projects. For that path, depth inside BuildPurdue and Repple is preferable to cross-corpus novelty.
- These are intentional refinements of the original guided-discovery idea, not accidental drift. The choices remain interests rather than links to static category lists, and the page still constructs one cumulative path.

## Good drift criteria
- Replacing the static four-category opening is legitimate if Karthik consciously prefers guided thematic discovery over immediate taxonomic glanceability.
- New visual behavior is legitimate when it makes the growing story easier to understand without increasing interface density.
- AI-generated connective language is legitimate only if it adds specific, source-grounded insight and does not become a narrator that speaks over Karthik's corpus.
- The interaction may evolve beyond the literal “choose your own adventure” metaphor if it preserves visitor-shaped, cumulative, coherent discovery.

## Agreement-drift risks
- Treating the exciting metaphor as proof that a quiz-like branching UI will be enjoyable.
- Hiding the breadth of Karthik's work so thoroughly that a visitor cannot tell what is available.
- Letting the accumulating page become long, repetitive, or visually maximalist.
- Offering choices that are merely renamed categories or filters rather than meaningful questions and tensions.
- Having TypeSafe select locally relevant items that repeat the same artifact, theme, or conclusion.
- Generating a smooth-sounding personal narrative that is less interesting than Karthik's real evidence and words.
- Mistaking more animations or unusual layout for meaningful dynamism.

## Ambiguities (resolved)
- Q: Does the journey replace the four glanceable categories?
  A: Yes. Thematic directions become the home-screen entry points.
- Q: How should the visitor express interest?
  A: By choosing the next direction; no separate like/dislike control is needed.
- Q: Should the journey become a complete map of the corpus?
  A: No. It should construct one coherent path.
