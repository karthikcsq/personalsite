# Discussion

## Current question
What modest, content-honest dynamic behavior can make a smaller portfolio feel alive without pretending it has enough breadth for a custom path or a TypeSafe-driven experience?

## Anchor-preserving points
- [anchor] Judge the interaction by how quickly it reveals important evidence about Karthik.
- [anchor] Require the connection to save effort or create a genuinely useful insight.
- [anchor] On a first scroll, visitors need identity, strongest work, and concrete evidence before they need relationships between artifacts.
- [anchor] A connection is useful only when its explanation reveals a repeated pattern, evolution, or contradiction that is not already obvious from the two titles.
- [anchor] The site should get its intelligence from choreography and selection, not from manufacturing variance the corpus does not contain.

## Interpretations
- [interpretation] “Someone scrolling through the site” means a visitor who has not yet committed to deep exploration and is deciding whether Karthik is worth more attention.
  - Alternative rejected: a highly engaged visitor already reading several full notes.

## Options
- [anchor] Remove attention gravity from the homepage and keep the first view direct and glanceable.
- [anchor] Reuse semantic connections as a second-layer continuation after someone finishes or meaningfully reads one artifact.
  - Show only one or two unusually strong connections.
  - State the human-readable reason and expose the supporting evidence.
- [anchor] Let attention select a manually authored thesis, then repaint all four categories as structured evidence for that thesis.
  - The AI ranks authentic theses and evidence; it does not write the visible claim.
- [anchor] Turn the sun into a chronological scrubber so the same minimal scene reveals how work, projects, and thinking evolved over time.
- [anchor] Use a weather shift to expose a real contradiction as two complete excerpts in tension, without resolving it for the visitor.
- [anchor] Keep the four category positions fixed while one evidence item under each changes in response to attention, preserving scanability while making the page adaptive.
- [anchor] Use a small curated set of real highlights, with controlled session variation, rather than an open-ended recommendation system.
- [expansion] Build a general semantic graph around the homepage categories.
  - Why it might be worth it: it visibly demonstrates an intelligent corpus and creates exploratory novelty.
  - Cost: interpretation overhead, visual competition, uncertain value to skimming visitors, and a higher burden of connection quality.

## Direction changes proposed
- [assistant-proposed] Treat semantic connection as a contextual continuation, not a homepage discovery mechanism.
  - Status: rejected
  - Reason: the behavior becomes valuable only after the visitor has an anchor and can understand why the connection matters.
- [user-proposed] Preserve the current minimalist environmental design and continue brainstorming dynamic behavior inside it.
  - Status: explicitly accepted
  - Reason: the user does not want to retreat to a conventional portfolio or settle on the proposed contextual-continuation pattern.
- [user-proposed] Drop the custom path and TypeSafe framing because the corpus lacks sufficient content variance.
  - Status: explicitly accepted
  - Reason: a smaller corpus should be edited and choreographed honestly instead of made to look broader through generated branching.

## Risks
- [risk] The visitor sees scattered related items but cannot tell why they matter.
- [risk] The interaction delays access to stronger, simpler portfolio content.
- [risk] The site communicates “AI is happening” more clearly than it communicates who Karthik is.
- [risk] Broad similarity produces plausible but shallow connections that feel like recommendations rather than insight.
- [risk] A custom path repeats the same small set of notes and makes the site feel less intelligent, not more.
