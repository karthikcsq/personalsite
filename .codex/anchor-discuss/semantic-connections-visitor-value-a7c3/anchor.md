## Domain
- product

## Starting intent
- Decide whether semantic connections materially improve a visitor's experience, rather than preserving the idea because it sounds technically impressive.
- Keep the website's original purpose visible: a smart visitor should quickly understand Karthik, discover substantive work and thinking, and feel that the interaction earns their attention.
- Continue exploring genuinely dynamic behavior inside the minimalist environmental design rather than retreating to a conventional portfolio or committing to contextual related links.
- Do not manufacture a custom choose-your-own-adventure path or add TypeSafe merely to make a smaller corpus appear more variable than it is.

## Goals
- Get an honest product judgment about visitor usefulness.
- Distinguish genuine discovery value from AI-flavored novelty.
- Brainstorm interactions where the page itself repaints or changes meaning while the four-category structure remains legible.

## Non-goals
- Defend the current implementation.
- Add complexity merely to demonstrate AI.
- Turn the discussion into an implementation plan before deciding whether the behavior deserves to exist.
- Pretend the corpus has enough breadth for a different, deeply personalized journey for every visitor.

## Constraints and taste
- Minimal, fast, and immediately legible.
- Visitors are likely skimming before they decide whether to invest attention.
- Dynamic behavior must reveal information they would not have found as easily in a normal portfolio.

## Good drift criteria
- A direction change is legitimate if it more directly helps visitors understand Karthik or discover unusually strong evidence from the corpus.

## Agreement-drift risks
- Keeping semantic connections because the assistant proposed them confidently.
- Mistaking visual cleverness or AI sophistication for usefulness.
- Making visitors learn an interaction before they receive value.

## Ambiguities (resolved)
- None yet.

## Ambiguities (unresolved)
- Whether the primary visitor is a recruiter, AI researcher, founder, collaborator, or a mixed audience.

- Scope: low
- Complexity: low
